# paper-drawer-edit (edit format)

**In plain words:** cards that look like paper slide in from the edge, like opening a drawer, each carrying one point.

**Use when:** the script is a set of points, steps or a comparison.

## Layout
- Off-white paper card with soft shadow, covers the lower 55% of a reel (face stays visible above) or slides fully over for a full-page moment.
- Card enters with slide-up (10 frames), next card pushes the old one out.
- One heading plus at most 3 short lines or one picture per card.
- Rough mix to aim for: face 20%, screen 40%, drawer 25%, full page 15%.

## Rules
- A card stays for the whole sentence it belongs to, then leaves on the cut.
- Only the original's name and screen-time split are public; the card design here is ours.
- Motion, colours and fonts come from mg-kit. Face placement comes from take-geometry.
- These measurements are a starting point written by us, not copied from a reference. Run learn-style on a reel the creator likes to replace them with measured numbers.
