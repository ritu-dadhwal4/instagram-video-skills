# caption-edit (edit format)

**In plain words:** just the creator talking, with big captions doing the visual work.

**Use when:** there is no screen or graphic to show; the words are the content.

## Layout
- Face full frame, eyes at about 38% from the top.
- Captions 2 to 4 words, centred, at about 62% from the top, below the chin.
- A slow push-in on each cut; alternate 100% and 108% framing between sentences to hide jump cuts.

## Rules
- One emoji or sticker per 5 seconds at most.
- The framing change happens ON the cut as a hard change, never as an animated zoom across it.
- Motion, colours and fonts come from mg-kit. Face placement comes from take-geometry.
- These measurements are a starting point written by us, not copied from a reference. Run learn-style on a reel the creator likes to replace them with measured numbers.
