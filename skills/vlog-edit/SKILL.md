# vlog-edit (edit format)

**In plain words:** a story of a day: many clips, music underneath, small titles for place and time.

**Use when:** footage is many short clips from different places, not one sitting take.

## Layout
- Order by story, not by clock: hook moment first, then the beginning.
- Clips 2 to 5 s, cut on the beat of the music where nobody is speaking.
- Small location/time title bottom-left for each new place.
- Captions only when someone speaks.

## Rules
- Music drops about 20 dB under speech.
- Match colour between clips shot on different cameras.
- Motion, colours and fonts come from mg-kit. Face placement comes from take-geometry.
- These measurements are a starting point written by us, not copied from a reference. Run learn-style on a reel the creator likes to replace them with measured numbers.
