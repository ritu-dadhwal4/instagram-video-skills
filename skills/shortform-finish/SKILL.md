# shortform-finish

**In plain words:** the last look before export. Is the first second interesting? Does anything drag? Do all the shots look the same colour?

## Checklist
1. Hook: something moves or appears in the first 0.5 s; the promise of the video is on screen by 2 s.
2. Pacing: no single picture stays unchanged for more than 4 s in a reel.
3. Colour: skin tone and brightness match between takes shot at different times.
4. Ending: last line is complete, then an end card or call to action of 1.5 s; no trailing silence.
5. Safe zones and face overlap re-checked on the final frames.
6. Length fits the platform target the creator gave.

## Rules
- Polish never changes the meaning or removes an approved line.
- Any timing change here sends the edit back through cut-audit and caption-align.
