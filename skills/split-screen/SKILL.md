# split-screen (edit format)

**In plain words:** two things at once: the creator in one half, the website they are talking about in the other.

**Use when:** The creator is explaining a tool, a site or steps on a screen.

## Layout
- Reel: screen on top 58%, face on bottom 42%, 6 px divider in a brand colour.
- Long-form: screen left 65%, face right 35%.
- Captions sit on the divider line.
- Full-face for the hook (first 2 s) and for opinions; split for anything being shown.

## Rules
- The screen half always shows what the current sentence is about.
- Switch between full-face and split with a hard cut.
- Motion, colours and fonts come from mg-kit. Face placement comes from take-geometry.
- These measurements are a starting point written by us, not copied from a reference. Run learn-style on a reel the creator likes to replace them with measured numbers.
