# design-asset (stage 4)

**In plain words:** the designer. Makes the cards and titles that appear on screen, like a card saying "$9" or a tool's logo with its name.

## Steps
1. Take the `card` rows from the asset plan.
2. Build each card as HTML/React using the mg-kit tokens, at 1080x1920 (reel) or 1920x1080 (long-form).
3. Render to PNG with transparent background where it sits over footage.
4. Open each PNG and look at it before using it.

## Rules
- Colours, fonts, card corners and highlight style come from `design.md` when the brief has one.
- Colours and fonts only from the creator's brand kit (`brand.md`). Never an off-palette colour.
- One idea per card, 7 words or fewer.
- Numbers and prices are copied from the checked asset plan, never retyped from memory.
- Third-party logos: official mark only, unaltered.
- Minimum text size 44 px on a 1080-wide frame.
