# broll-style-edit (edit format)

**In plain words:** The creator's voice, but the picture is all supporting footage. No face.

**Use when:** it is a voice-over, or the camera footage is unusable.

## Layout
- A new picture every 2 to 3 seconds, each matching the line.
- Captions centred in the lower third on every line.
- Slow push-in or pan on stills so nothing is frozen.

## Rules
- Every clip comes from broll-select, screen-capture or genvideo with its source recorded.
- No clip repeats within one video.
- Motion, colours and fonts come from mg-kit. Face placement comes from take-geometry.
- These measurements are a starting point written by us, not copied from a reference. Run learn-style on a reel the creator likes to replace them with measured numbers.
