# layered-head-edit (edit format)

**In plain words:** the background is removed and the creator stands in front of whatever they are talking about, like a weather presenter.

**Use when:** The creator should stay visible while the thing behind them changes.

## Layout
- Cut-out head and shoulders, lower third of the frame, 45 to 55% of the width, on the side opposite their gaze.
- Background layer: screen recording, card or b-roll, slightly dimmed (about 15%).
- Thin soft shadow under the cut-out so it does not look pasted.

## Rules
- Check the cut-out edge on hair in 5 frames; a flickering edge means use split-screen instead.
- Nothing important in the background sits behind the creator.
- Motion, colours and fonts come from mg-kit. Face placement comes from take-geometry.
- These measurements are a starting point written by us, not copied from a reference. Run learn-style on a reel the creator likes to replace them with measured numbers.
