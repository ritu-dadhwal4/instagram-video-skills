# Design file (design.md)

Copy this to `design.md` next to your videos, or let reel-edit write it for you from a theme or from your reference pictures. Every card, caption, highlight and end card reads its colours and fonts from here.

## Colours (hex codes)
- Background: #FFFFFF
- Text: #0A0A0A
- Accent (the one highlight colour): #FFD400
- Text on the accent: #3A2C00
- Card background: #F5F5F5

## Fonts (free Google Fonts, or a font file you own)
- Headings and cards: Geist, weight 800
- Captions: Geist, weight 900, italic
- Code or typed text: Geist Mono

## Shapes
- Card corners: 24 px
- Divider between face and screen: 6 px, accent colour
- Highlight: accent underline, or accent outline box for links

## Captions
- How the words arrive: Karaoke line
- Look: Strong (heavy italic, dark outline + soft shadow), spoken word in the accent colour

## Where this came from
- Theme / reference pictures / brand guide: Clean White theme
