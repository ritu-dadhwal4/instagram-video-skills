# sfx-design (stage 7)

**In plain words:** small sounds make motion feel real: a "whoosh" when a card slides in, a "pop" when a word appears. This skill decides which sound, when, and how loud.

## Steps
1. List every motion in the edit (from mg-kit moves) with its frame.
2. Match sound to motion: slide/wipe → whoosh, pop-in → pop/click, highlight → pencil/tick, number reveal → soft ding.
3. Put the sound's loudest moment on the frame where the motion is fastest, not where it starts.
4. Add music if wanted; lower it whenever the creator speaks.

## Rules
- Sound follows something that MOVES. No motion, no sound.
- Levels: voice is the reference. SFX about 12 dB below voice, music about 20 dB below. *Why: effects that compete with the voice are the most common first-draft complaint.*
- Never the same effect twice in a row; rotate 2 or 3 variants.
- At most one effect every 1.5 seconds.
- Only sounds with a licence recorded in `assets/sfx/SOURCES.md`.
